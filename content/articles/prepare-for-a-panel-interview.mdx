A panel is not a test of whether you can guess every question. It is a chance to show how you listen, structure decisions and work with people whose priorities differ.

## Prepare a useful story bank

Bring concise examples about a difficult project, a measurable result, an error you corrected and a time you worked across functions. Then connect each example directly to the role.
