Negotiation works best when it is prepared, specific and respectful. Research salary ranges, define your priorities and leave enough space for the employer to respond.

## Name the value, not just the number

Connect your request to responsibilities, scarce skills and the impact you expect to deliver. Consider the full offer, including health cover, growth and flexibility.
