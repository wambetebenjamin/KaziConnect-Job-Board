Good remote work is a set of repeatable agreements with yourself and the people you work with. Make your availability visible, protect focused time and write down decisions.

## Design for connection

Use regular short check-ins, clear project updates and a reliable way to raise blockers. The goal is not more meetings; it is less ambiguity.
