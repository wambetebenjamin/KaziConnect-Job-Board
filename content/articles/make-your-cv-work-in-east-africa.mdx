A strong CV helps a recruiter understand your direction in under a minute. Start with a clear headline that names the work you do and the kind of contribution you make.

## Put evidence next to every claim

Replace generic phrases with evidence: scope, outcomes, tools and teams. *Reduced supplier turnaround from five days to two* says much more than *excellent communicator*.

## Make the first page do the work

Use a readable font, honest dates and a simple chronological structure. Save portfolio links, references and supporting documents for the right places in the hiring process.
